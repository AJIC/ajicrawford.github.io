Scanned picture postcard c. 1900 of an Ussuri brown bear being guided into a heperset, where it will be raised prior to being ceremonially killed as part of the iomante, an Ainu ceremony. One of a set of Ainu picture postcards acquired in Jimbōchō from Shinsendō Books, July 2026.
